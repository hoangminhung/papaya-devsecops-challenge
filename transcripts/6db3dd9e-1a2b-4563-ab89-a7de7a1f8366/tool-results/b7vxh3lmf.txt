     3	## English summary
     4	
     5	- **Purpose:** fix exactly what is submitted before the final writing pass. No new design decisions here;
     6	  this note only decides form, order, length and scope.
     7	- **Docs (§1):** a one-page `README.md`, then numbered docs: framework (trimmed from ~11k to ~9.5–10k words by
     8	  moving the rollout and evidence detail out, keeping a one-line evidence summary per layer), a new short
     9	  engineer guide, rollout plan 30/60/90, a compliance "control and evidence register", the existing threat
    10	  model and problem→control map, the 10 existing ADRs, and three Mermaid diagrams.
    11	- **Writing order (§2):** change workflow (layer 3) and identity & access (layer 1) first, each written
    12	  together with its examples; layer 5 is frozen input for them; supporting docs after; README last.
    13	- **Examples (§3):** 8 core and 3 secondary artifacts, each tied to a framework claim and a local check whose
    14	  limits are stated. Main pushback on the initial idea: "environment approval" does not exist for private
    15	  repos on GitHub Team (R3.1.2), so the gate example is a **ruleset on `main`** plus an environment limited to
    16	  `main` without reviewers; an IAM permission set alone proves little, so the OIDC trust policy (immutable
    17	  `sub` format) and an SCP are added; the risk stop, the least proven part of the design, gets a runnable
    18	  classifier; "CrossGuard" is now called Pulumi Policies and is a phase-2, secondary item.
    19	- **Excluded (§4):** anything that would need invented facts (k0s, Firebase, GCP), running tools against real
    20	  targets, implementations of open gaps, new decisions, and — pending the owner — a managed hook example.
    21	- **Open questions (§5):** framework length, engineer guide, renaming docs, examples scope, installing
    22	  linters/packages, dropping hooks from framing Q14, status of the problem→control map.
    23	- Reviewed once by an independent agent (1 HIGH, 9 MEDIUM, all fixed; log in §6).
